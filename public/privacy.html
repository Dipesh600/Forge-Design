Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of Your information when You use the FORGE AI Design Studio Android application (the "Service") and tells You about Your privacy rights and how the law protects You.

1. Information We Collect

While using Our Service, We may ask You to provide Us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify You. Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:

  • Email address (via Google Sign-in / Firebase Authentication)
  • Usage Data (automatically collected via Firebase Analytics)
  • Prompts and messages you send to the AI Design Agents

2. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

The Service uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify You. We use Firebase (a Google service) for backend infrastructure, authentication, and analytics. The third-party service providers used by the app include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Analytics)

Your chat history and generated design assets are stored via Firebase Firestore and Firebase Storage associated with your account to allow your work to persist across sessions.

3. Data Safety and Security

The security of Your Personal Data is important to Us. Your communications with our backend systems and AI models are encrypted in transit over HTTPS. Furthermore, your data stored in our database is secured by Firebase security rules to ensure only authenticated users can access their own projects.

4. Data Deletion and Your Rights

You have the right to request the deletion of your Personal Data that We have collected about You. You can delete your account and associated project data directly within the application settings, or you can contact us to request manual deletion.

5. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update Our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify You of any changes by publishing the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
